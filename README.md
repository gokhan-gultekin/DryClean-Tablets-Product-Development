# DryClean-Tablets-Product-Development
DryClean Tablets – Market Research & Product Strategy

Consumer Research | Product Development | Market Segmentation | Go-to-Market Strategy

**Project Overview**

DryClean Tablets is a conceptual waterless laundry cleaning solution developed as part of an academic team project at Bilkent University.

The project explores the market potential of an alternative laundry product through consumer research, competitive analysis, customer segmentation, product positioning, and marketing strategy development.

The objective was to understand consumer needs, identify potential target markets, and develop a research-informed go-to-market strategy for a new consumer product.

**Research Objectives**

The project focused on four key business questions:

What factors influence consumers when selecting laundry cleaning products?

How receptive are consumers to an alternative waterless laundry solution?

Which customer segments offer the strongest potential for market entry?

How should the product be positioned, priced, and distributed?

Research Methodology

The research combined secondary market analysis with primary consumer research.

Primary Research

Conducted a consumer survey with 56 respondents.

Used a 21-question questionnaire covering purchasing behavior, product preferences, willingness to switch, pricing sensitivity, and demographics.

Analyzed responses to identify consumer priorities and potential adoption barriers.

Secondary Research

Market environment and competitive analysis

Consumer behavior and demographic analysis

Customer segmentation and target market assessment

Product positioning and marketing mix development

Strategic Implications

Product Value Proposition

Cleaning effectiveness emerged as the leading purchase consideration. Therefore, the proposed product positioning should emphasize reliable cleaning performance alongside convenience and environmental benefits.

Customer Adoption

Survey responses indicated substantial openness to switching products if DryClean Tablets proved effective. This supports prioritizing product demonstrations, trial opportunities, and evidence of performance in a potential launch strategy.

Distribution Strategy

Supermarkets were the most frequently reported purchasing channel among respondents, suggesting that physical retail distribution should be considered alongside online channels.

Target Market & Product Positioning

The marketing strategy considered both consumer and institutional markets.

B2C Segments

Environmentally conscious consumers

Younger consumers open to alternative cleaning solutions

Convenience-oriented consumers seeking practical laundry products

Potential B2B Segments

Hotels and accommodation providers

Universities and residential institutions

Hospitals and healthcare facilities

The proposed positioning combines convenience, sustainability, and cleaning effectiveness to differentiate DryClean Tablets from conventional laundry products.
